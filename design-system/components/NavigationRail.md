# NavigationRail

Side navigation for three to seven destinations on medium and expanded windows (M3).

- Same `items` / `value` contract as NavigationBar. `menu` adds a menu button (`onMenu` opens the SideNav); `fab` takes FAB props.
- 80px wide on `surface`, pinned to the leading edge.
- From 600px to 1199px; from `window-large` use Header with SideNav.

## Sound

Silent (`sound-navigate`).
