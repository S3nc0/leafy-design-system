# TopAppBar

The bar at the top of a screen on compact and medium windows (M3).

- Variants: `small` (64px), `center`, `medium` (title below in `headline-small`), `large`.
- Provide `title`, optional `navigationIcon` with `navigationLabel` and `onNavigate`, and up to three `actions`.
- `scrolled` switches the fill to `surface-container`.
- From `window-large` up, use Header with SideNav instead.
